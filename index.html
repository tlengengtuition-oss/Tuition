About Me

A passion for teaching, a track record of results.

Hi, I'm T-Leng (Mr Eng to my students) — a kind, patient and dedicated private tutor based in Singapore with over 5 years of experience helping students across all levels reach their academic potential. I graduated from Singapore Management University with a Bachelor of Science in Information Systems, and have since worked closely with families across Singapore.

I believe in motivating students through engaging lessons and building their self-confidence. My approach focuses on personalised guidance — helping students not only understand the subject matter, but also develop independent learning skills that will serve them well beyond the classroom.

Whether you need intensive exam preparation, consistent weekly support, or help building a stronger foundation, I'm here to help every step of the way.

Before You Start

Questions parents ask

What if we need to cancel or reschedule a lesson?
  • 24 hours’ notice or more: no charge, and the lesson is rescheduled.
  • Less than 24 hours: still no charge if you move the lesson to another slot in the same month (up to twice a month), switch it to an online lesson, or it’s a medical or family emergency. Otherwise 50% of the fee applies.
  • Less than 1 hour, or no-show: the full fee applies.
Please give notice by WhatsApp to +65 9186 6928.
What if you are late or need to cancel?
Any time I lose is made up in full: by extending the lesson, adding it to the next one, or refunding it, whichever suits you. If I have to cancel, the lesson is rescheduled or credited in full on the next invoice.
How does payment work?
Fees are agreed when lessons start and confirmed in writing. You’re billed monthly after the month’s lessons, payable by PayNow within a week. Fees are reviewed once a year and when your child moves up a level, with at least two weeks’ notice.
Are there lessons on public holidays and school holidays?
No lessons on public holidays unless we both agree; make-ups are arranged together. During school holidays your regular slot continues unless you tell me otherwise. For family trips, give two weeks’ notice and there’s no charge.
Do you teach online?
Yes, on Zoom or Google Meet, at the same fee as a home lesson. Switching a lesson online is also the easiest way to avoid a late-cancellation charge.
What do we need at home?
A quiet table with good lighting, with the TV and phones off. For students under 18, a parent or another responsible adult should be at home during the lesson.
Are notes and worksheets included?
Yes. My notes, worksheets and practice papers are included in the fee, for your child’s own use only.
How will I know how my child is doing?
I share a progress update after each school assessment. Sharing school papers and results helps me decide what to teach next. I won’t promise a specific grade — results depend on your child’s effort too — but I will always give you honest feedback.
How do we pause or stop lessons?
Either of us can stop or pause with two weeks’ notice. After that the weekly slot is released, so it may not be free if you come back later.
